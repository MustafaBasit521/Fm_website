About the studio

Hi, I'm Ayesha.

I learned my first chain stitch from my Nani one winter in Lahore, using a hook she'd kept in her sewing tin for thirty years. What began as a way to slow down became Bundle of Loops — a one-woman studio and, now, a little shop.

Every flower, keychain and car hanger is made by me, by hand, from start to finish. Most of what I make today started as someone's idea in my DMs — a team jersey, a favourite colour, a gift for a sister.

Ayesha
