Bundle of Loops

Handmade crochet from Lahore — your idea, our crochet. The system is built from natural yarn tones, an editorial serif, and one stitched detail: a dashed "running stitch" line used for dividers and focus. One accent, madder red, is reserved for the moments that matter — buying, publishing, confirming.
